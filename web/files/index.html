    Files and settings¶

    A loop's Files tab browses its folder; Settings holds its preferences and its problem.

    Files¶

    Four views, each with a count: Workbench, Library, Inputs, All files. On a phone a file opens on its own, with ‹ Back.

    Workbench¶

    The agents' own folder, workbench/. Only for people with the Internals permission (Admin › People › Users); for others it is hidden and left out of a Full export.

    • A search over names and first lines.
    • Overview: workbench/README.md, opened first.
    • Tools: five shown, "+N" for more.
    • Notes by age: Today, Yesterday, This week, Earlier (folded).
    • Numbered notes: three or more files of one name and a number (draft1297-…) folded into one row.
    • Other: the rest.

    Files open in the same viewer as All files.

    Library¶

    What the agents can read: the loop's library/, one row per document (kind, title, size), ✓ digested, or not relevant when its digest says so.

    • N of M digested, a search, ↻ (read again), {} (show digests).
    • + Add a document: files go to library/, a folder to library/<folder>/, a single .zip is unpacked.
    • Each linked project's library follows, read-only (a lock), at library/<project>@<group>/.
    • Also digested: digests of other files (notes, knowledge files) and of files now gone.
    • Delete digest (the bin; needs Edit; audited). The group head deletes digests of missing files (Delete N digests). A digest of a file still there is made again at the next read.
    • Opening a document shows Download, Raw, Actions, its Digest and Outline (as the agents read it).

    Inputs¶

    Everything else the loop was given:

    • In the problem: files and folders the document names (golden model, command files, library entries);
    • Skills: skills/ or the document's skills folder;
    • Other.

    A lock marks hidden files. The foot links to Settings › Problem.

    Large folders¶

    • A big workbench folder is one row (data/, 12345 files, newest change).
    • Lists show the newest and count the rest ("102 more · All files").
    • Not walked: links, other mounted file systems, dot folders, __pycache__, .gitignored paths.
    • All files loads a big folder as you scroll ("100 of 12345 entries").
    • The configurator's Files and Library folds big folders too; library/, skills/ and skill folders are never folded.

    All files¶

    The folder as it is: the document, scripts, library/, workbench, out/, runs/.

    • Open a file to view, edit, save or download; Raw and Fullscreen show it whole.
    • Upload: drop files or folders, or use Files (upload arrow) and Folder (folder with a plus).
    • Actions: Rename, Move, Delete, Hide from AI / Show to AI (edits knowledge.hidden; Hidden from AI). Hidden items show a lock; hover says hidden from AI or what hides it.
    • A name with @ at the top of library/ is refused (reserved for project libraries).
    • Linked projects' libraries appear under library/, marked project · read-only. Change them on the project's page.
    • .gitignored files are hidden; .gitignore'd and dot files lists them greyed.
    • Images (PNG, JPEG, GIF, WebP) are shown; SVG stays text. PDFs open in the browser's viewer (Firefox inline; Chrome, Edge and phones get Open and Download).

    Settings¶

    Two parts: Preferences and Problem.

    Preferences¶

    • Measurements: per metric: shown, main (in lists and summaries), shown as change from the baseline. Charts and the decision always use every measurement.
    • Environment variables: the loop's own, over its projects' (Groups › Variables), your account's and the server's. Applied at the next start.
    • Sharing: a grid of who has which words (the words).
      • Rows: Owner (fixed), then each user or group shared with.
      • Level: Viewer, Asker, Contributor, Maintainer, or Custom.
      • Words: view (always on), ask (chat about it), edit (files, document, settings, notes, accept Chat proposals), run (start, stop, restart, re-measure), share.
      • People adds a user or group as Viewer. Ticking a box saves.
      • A sharer who is not the owner can give at most what they hold.
      • Linked projects show read-only rows (via project); ▸ lists their people; Change on the project › edits them.
      • Your rights are your row, your groups' rows and your projects', together. Being in a group with the owner gives nothing.
      • A run started by anyone runs as the owner, on the owner's settings and limits.
    • Projects: the projects the loop is linked into, in order (drag, or the chevrons). The order sets the libraries' order. Per row: Learns (may the project learn from runs) and ✕.
      • Add to a project… lists projects where you hold link, and global ones that take loops.
      • Linking gives the project's members their loop words (never share). A global project gives no rights; its learning starts off.
    • Name & ownership: rename (1 to 60 letters, digits, _, -) or transfer. The loop must be stopped with nothing reading it. Everything moves with it; the old address stops working. A transfer drops the admin's overrides, shares and agent tests.
    • Advanced (admins): sandbox on/off; memory, CPU, process and scratch limits (clamped to the server's maximum, Sandbox limits); parallel work; extra hosts; raw TCP/UDP; host folders mounted; extra Nixpkgs or Nixchip packages. Others see a summary line.
    • Maintenance (folded): Clean disk (scratch, old pass folders, sandbox cache), Condense history (log, journal, transcript), Compact and check (records, document).
    • Record (folded): Prune stale records, Prune empty starts, Prune failed designs, Invalidate all, Re-Evaluate (Results). Pending requests show here and on the Overview with Cancel.
    • Archive, reset or delete (loop stopped; cannot be undone):
      • Archive: read-only, disk freed (Archive).
      • Reset: clears what runs generated; you choose what to keep (workbench, results and history, caches). Document, files and settings stay.
      • Delete: removes the loop.

    Archive¶

    Archive (owner or admin) asks for the loop's name and lists what goes and stays, with sizes.

    Kept Deleted
    The results, out/: designs, measurements, decisions (Results and its exports) The passes' working files
    The document, your files and the library The tools' whole outputs (Live keeps each one's end)
    The workbench's notes and tools The sandbox's tool caches and scratch
    The last start's log, the answer, notes and Chat Work in progress and build folders
    What Live shows of the past starts The log of the starts before the last
    • Overview, Live, Results, Files and Chat still read it. Edits, starts and resets are refused until Unarchive.
    • Archived loops show under the Archived chip; search and links still find them.
    • Clean-ups skip them. A start after Unarchive rebuilds scratch.

    Export and import¶

    Export (owner, editors, admin) writes one .zip, for this or another Flux:

    Loop only Full
    The problem document, your files, lib/, skills/, the library yes yes
    Hidden files (listed in the file's manifest) yes yes
    The results, out/: designs, measurements, decisions, ideas yes
    The workbench's notes and tools (no build folders), the answer, the notes yes
    • Never exported: variables and secrets, credentials, agent instructions, shares, rights, project libraries, Chat conversations, Live history, agent working files, tool outputs, caches, logs, links, repositories.
    • The file starts with a manifest: kind, maker, server, date, documents, hidden paths, command count, sizes. Audited ("loop exported").

    Import replaces this loop's contents (loop stopped).

    1. Pick the file. Its manifest shows, with what will be replaced ("This replaces x's document, files and library, and its results: 12 designs are replaced by 40 designs.").
    2. Replace. A Full file also brings its record, workbench, answer and notes.

    3. A Loop only file clears existing results unless you tick Keep the current results.

    4. The loop keeps its name, id, owner, shares, projects, variables, settings, Chat and run history.

    5. The Overview says "Replaced from x.full.zip, made by ada@host, date, Full"; an editor can clear it (×). Audited ("loop replaced").
    6. Nothing runs on import.
    7. Refused, changing nothing: files before Nov 12, 2026 (names the Flux to convert it with), a document with old box names (generate:, orchestrate:, ...) or params: (convert in Flux fdfd1faf0, Server › Old data), files from a newer Flux ("update this server first").
    8. Limits (admin settings): loop_import_max (2 GB file), loop_import_unpacked_max (16 GB unpacked).

    Problem¶

    Viewers without edit see the configurator's steps beside the document, read-only.

    • Configurator: the document in six steps (try it in the Preview's Create a loop guide). See below.
    • Direct edit: the YAML, saved with its diff. A document that does not load is flagged before saving.
    • Agent: describe a change; an agent revises a copy and shows the diff (not while running).
    • Check: checks the document (ready or not, refusals, missing files, checks, stages, objectives, tools, model), as Start does; it runs no design. Full output under Details.
    • Several problems (problem.yaml and each top-level NAME.problem.yaml; foo.yaml is an input): a menu picks which one to edit (settings/problem/edit?doc=fast.problem.yaml). One that does not load is red and opens in Direct edit. Which problem runs is picked in Start.

    The configurator¶

    • Save shows a diff. What the form cannot express is kept as written, greyed at the end of the document, and listed on Save. Keys the loader ignores (a misspelled timout_s) are kept for you to fix.
    • Save counts the document as checked once Check passed on these files, or a start ran.
    • Steps are cards of rows: label, then box or menu; a greyed value is the default. The line at the step's foot explains the focused setting.
    • Prompt: name, design file, what to make, rules, Files.
    • Check & Measure: Checks and Measurements cards.
    • Objective: how designs are compared, then the numbers.
    • Extra: Parameters, Running, Resources (folded cards; one off its default opens).
    • Graph: the loop's boxes. Left of the ring, Setup (Plan, Baseline) and Knowledge (Skills, Library, Workbench, Insights, Ideas, Notes, Hidden). Solid outline: on; dashed: off. An AI pill marks FluxAgent or an agent at work.

    A box's panel starts with By: off (where allowed), agent, or the box's own kind (rules, script, files, goals).

    • With agent: an Agent menu (FluxAgent, then the server's agents), Thinking and effort. An agent the server does not offer shows (not available), and the loop will not start until fixed.
    • A coding agent's options fold under <agent> · options: Questions, Wait, Time limit and last turn, May time out, Session.
    • Session: one per pass (default) or one per loop (with turns, or Until stopped: turns: forever). Design's: one per design, warm start (default; Study time in minutes, seed_timeout_s), one per design, cold start (session: fresh), or one per loop.
    • Design's Probe card: on/off, which gate checks, gate and measure runs.
    • Search's Policy card: Policy, then Search (an algorithm or AI) and its settings.
    • Limits (folded): prototype limits, FluxAgent's tool turns.
    • Check and Measure link to where their commands are set. Choose's By is goals or agent (breaks ties).
    • Knowledge bubbles: Skills on/off and list; Library off or who sums up papers; Workbench off or who sums it up; Insights off, agent or rules; Ideas off or who cleans up; Notes on/off; Hidden paths.
    • A dot marks a value the document sets.

    Issues (what Check and Direct edit report: an error stops the loop, a warning is an ignored key) show where they are set:

    • the rail counts them ("● 1 error · 1 warning"; Save: "1 to fix · 2 warnings");
    • the Graph box wears the mark and shows the value as written (codx);
    • the panel outlines the setting with plain words, "Did you mean X?", a fix (Use codex, Remove) and Open line N. A fix is written to the file (refused if it changed meanwhile).
