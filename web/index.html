    Tour¶

    flux serve is a shared server with accounts. Each user has loops; each loop is a document and its files, and runs on the server, sandboxed. You check, start and stop loops, follow them live, and read their results in the browser. Install starts one.

    Loops¶

    The first page lists your loops: their state, their designs as Results' tabs count them -- "2 / 12 / 30" is 2 accepted this start, 12 accepted, 30 in All (the accepted and the rejected), its tooltip adding the failed and pending ones -- and the decision's number on the first objective (✓ or ✗ against its limit). The Overview's Acceptance rate is the same share: the Accepted tab's designs of All. Beside its state, each loop names its design agent, the one that works its generate box and the slowest part of a pass: FluxAgent, claude, codex, opencode or an agent your admin added. It is tinted while the loop runs and plain while it rests. A document saved in Flux names its new agent at once; one changed another way (an agent, the command line) within a moment while some page looks at the loop. Search, filter by state, and sort by activity, name or decision. Each state in the filter is led by the mark its rows' pill draws, in its tone: Running the turning ring, Idle, Never run and Archived a ring, Failed a cross; All only its word. Shared with me lists loops others shared with you or your group, with what you may do in a few words ("ask · edit · run"). Archived loops are under their own Archived chip (Archive).

    The list is drawn at once from the loops' states; a loop whose numbers changed shows a small turning ring in its Designs and Best cells until they come, a moment later.

    When you see a group's project, the list opens with a row of project tabs above the search: All, one per group project and In no project, each with its count beside it in grey. The chosen tab is bold and underlined. Under the tabs, the search takes the row's room and the state filter sits beside it. A count of 0 is dimmed, in the tabs and in the state filter, and still a choice: a project with no loop yet says "No loops yet" with a link to the project, where you link one. With many projects the tabs scroll sideways, a fade on the side that hides more; on a phone the search takes its own line and the state filter scrolls under it. The tab you chose is kept in this browser across reloads; the arrow keys move between tabs. When you see no group project, there are no tabs. The list stays flat, each loop once: while All is chosen, a Project column names each loop's first group project (a link to it) and "+N" its others (Groups).

    New loop¶

    New loop offers five ways to make one:

    Way What it does
    Configurator A form in six steps, the same as the Loop crafter
    Create or upload An empty loop, or one from a folder, files or a .zip you drop or pick, or from a problem document pasted (Paste YAML)
    Direct edit Write the YAML yourself
    Agent An agent writes the loop from your prompt and files (Write a loop with an agent)
    Clone A copy of an existing loop, with its problem but none of its runs

    A loop's page¶

    A loop has six tabs, and its run buttons in its header: Start (▶) at rest, Stop (■) and Restart (↻) joined while it runs, each with its word, beside the header's other buttons. The same buttons are at the end of each row of the Loops list as small square icons; hover one (or focus it) for what it does.

    Tab What it shows
    Overview Its state, the decision and its objectives, the best designs, a best-so-far chart, a note to the running loop and its agent's question
    Live What the run does now, every pass of it, as a graph or a timeline (Live)
    Results Every design measured, charts, ideas and the library (Results)
    Agents Every model and agent turn, newest first and a page at a time as you scroll: where it was taken (pass, box, try) and how its try came out, prompt, reply, tool calls, time and tokens; a turn of a box whose sessions are kept across passes says its lane and "resumed (turn k of K)" or "new session" with why
    Files The loop's files and the agents' workbench (Files and settings)
    Settings Preferences, sharing, maintenance, and the problem itself (Files and settings)

    An agent that writes or revises the loop's problem shows its strip at the top of the Overview while it works, and after it ends until something else has taken over: the loop started, the document changed, the draft made a loop, or six hours gone. Hide from Overview on an ended strip hides it for good, on every device, until the next agent job. Its history stays in Settings › Problem › Agent.

    The Overview draws each card as soon as what it shows is read: the state at once, the notes, the workbench and the agents' usage next, the cards of results (the decision, the charts, the recent designs) once the results' summary comes, each a placeholder meanwhile (its title and a few grey lines, a small card its label and a turning ring). The summary is kept by the server and made again as each pass ends; while a loop runs, a look between two of its passes may show it as it was a moment ago, read again a second later. Settings and Live read the same summary; only Results reads every row.

    The Decision card is one line, then the numbers: the decision's name and its pill (✓ accepted; ! closest while no design meets every requirement, ✗ failed), "ties with #229" when another accepted design has its very numbers, and "last pass 17 min ago" at the right; hover the pill for why it was chosen -- the rule and its limits ("Decided by: smallest area_um2 at fmax_mhz >= 1000") and the stage it was measured at. Under it a big tile for each main measurement (Settings › Measurements' Main; each objective when none is main), its change from the reference in percent (the latest baseline at its stage, else the accepted designs' P10 for an objective minimized, P90 for one maximized; its value when there is none), green or red by its limit, then Best 3: the rank, the design (the decision with its orange trophy) and each main measurement's change (each objective's when none is main), a row opening Results. The decision says one percent on its tile, its Best 3 row, Results and the Loops list: every one is compared with the same reference. Results › opens Results; the table's own tools (columns, Relative) are Results'. Best draws a chart per main measurement under one legend for the card (Baseline, accepted, failed, decision), each titled by its best and its change from the baseline -- "best cycles (-12.9%)", or "best cycles 6189" with no baseline; fullscreen the same, larger. The Overview's charts (Best, the Pareto front) draw a starred design as any other point, with no Starred in the legend: the stars are Results › Graphs' and the tables'.

    Starred designs is a card you add yourself (Account › Customize, a column's Add menu; it is in no default layout): a tile for each design starred by hand on this loop, the latest starred first, three to a row (one on a phone). A tile names the design with its marks (the decision's trophy, the hand star) and its standing under it (decision, accepted, failed, ...), then each main measurement's change from the reference in big figures with its value under it -- red where it misses a limit, green where it is better. The decision's tile is edged in Flux's orange. A click opens the design in Results; Results › opens the table. With nothing starred the card says how: open a design in Results and press Star. Who starred a design and when is in Results (hover its star), not on the card.

    The Overview reads only what its cards show: a card your layout does not show costs nothing.

    Small cards. The Overview's top row shows five by default: State, Designs measured, Passes, Last improvement and Working now. Account › Customize (the Overview layout) adds, removes and orders them -- Agents, Objective, the tokens, cost and the others under "Add small card" -- up to 10 in all ("7 of 10" beside the list), or none; past five they wrap onto a second row (five to a row on a wide screen, three on a narrower one, two on a phone). Defaults in the editor gives the five back; a layout you saved keeps its cards. Hover a card for what its number is and what it is made of. The newer ones:

    Card Shows
    Last improvement When the best design by the loop's objectives (the first, then the next on a tie; the decision when no objective ranks) last got better -- "42 min ago" -- the design, the pass it was made in, and the passes ended since with no better one
    Pass speed The median wall time of the latest 20 passes ended (a pass's first task to its last), how many ran at once at most, and passes an hour over that stretch
    Working now The agents and models at work this moment in the running start, and each box's designs at work ("11 design · 3 check · 2 measure"); "idle" when the loop is not running
    Idea queue The ideas queued for testing, those with an evaluation, and those proposed and neither tested nor queued
    Uptime How long this start has run, and the starts today (its tooltip says how many followed another, by your clock)
    One metric One metric's best among the accepted designs and its change from the baseline ("−12.9%", "best 6189 vs baseline 7105"; a percentile of the accepted designs when there is no baseline). Its metric is the one you type under the small cards in the layout editor -- in every loop that measures it -- else each loop's first objective
    Stage funnel How far the designs got, each at its latest attempt: made → passed the checks → measured on the last stage (each stage in the tooltip)

    Last improvement, One metric and Stage funnel are made by the server with the summary, for the cards your layout shows; Pass speed, Working now and Uptime from the running start's journal and the loop's starts, in a small read of their own that a running loop's Overview makes again every few seconds -- a layout without them reads neither.

    Start asks for the passes (or "until I stop it") and whether to stop at the first measurement. A trunk document's start says what runs under the passes, which count its hand-ins: "trunk: 2 agents (claude (opus), codex), 3 branches at once, 6 may wait" (each agent's model when its document says one). A loop of several problems (problem.yaml and each NAME.problem.yaml) asks which: every one is listed, one that does not load shown but not to pick, said under the menu with why and a Direct edit link that opens it in Settings › Problem; the menu shows as soon as there are two, even when only one loads, and starts on the one last started (on the first that loads when that one does not). It runs flux task check first when the inputs changed; if the check fails, the button reads Start anyway. A start resumes from the record, and the page stays where it is: started from a row of the Loops list (or Admin › Loops), the list stays, the row turns running and a notice says Started with an Open link to the loop, so several loops start one after another without leaving the list; started from a loop's header, the tab you are on stays. A refused start says why where you pressed it. A failed loop's Start says Start again; while an agent is still writing the problem, Start waits.

    Stop and Restart take effect after the current pass. Once one is asked, only that button is tinted red, with a small hourglass (after its word in the header, in its corner in a row), and the page says when it happens ("Stops after pass 4"). The loop keeps its blue running pill -- it still runs until the pass ends -- and a small amber pill before it shows the stop's square or the restart's arrow; point at it to read "Stops after pass 4" or "Restarts after pass 4", in the header and in the Loops list alike. The other button still works, so a stop can become a restart and back. Hover or focus the tinted button and it turns solid red: press it again to do it now. It asks first, then abandons the pass, and the button stays solid red until the process ends.

    A loop's Chat¶

    Chat, on every tab of a loop, opens a panel with one conversation about the loop (it was called Talk before; old #/app/NAME/ask and #/app/NAME/talk links still open it, and #/app/NAME/chat/ID opens on that conversation). Its one bar at the top holds the conversation's name, which opens the list of the loop's conversations, by when each last moved, with + New chat, a spinner on one being answered, and the more menu's Delete where you may; then Open in Chat (the open icon, beside Close in one toolbar), which opens the conversation in the top bar's Chat, with its Settings and the loop's Chat workbench; then Close. While the loop's agent asks you something, the Chat button has a dot in the agent's colour; pointing at it shows the question. Below is that one conversation and its message box: Ask puts a question (why it stalls, which design is best, what to try next) to an agent or FluxAgent, which reads the loop and its results and changes nothing. Thinking & commands under an answer shows how it was made. In the message box Enter sends and Shift+Enter starts a new line. The loop's chats share a Chat workbench, where their agents keep tools, notes and attached files, for those who may edit the loop: a file an answer wrote, listed under it, opens in Chat's page (A loop's Chat workbench). It is not the loop's own workbench/, and the loop's runs never see it.

    The Overview has a card for Note to the loop: while the loop runs, a note you send there reaches the loop's agents at its next step. Its box is one line, as Chat's: Enter sends (the arrow at its end too), Shift+Enter adds a line; a loop at rest has no box (nothing would read the note before its next start), the list stays. The notes sent are listed under it, newest first, one line each -- these are the loop's latest notes (there is no separate Latest notes card): a dot, the note's text and how long ago it was sent. The dot is filled once the loop has read the note, as its record says ("Read at pass 14", or "Read" when the pass is not known: passes at once, or an R pass), and a ring while it waits for the loop's next step. A note cut to its line opens whole when pressed. The latest three show, and N more the rest of the latest twenty. Whoever may edit the loop removes one with its bin (on hover, always on a touch screen): a note the loop has not read yet never reaches it, and one it read is not read again.

    When the loop's agent asks something, the Agent asks card shows its question with its choices, You decide and a free answer; your answer goes as a note. Under it are the questions already answered, newest first, one line each: the question, your answer or pick, and how long ago -- the latest three, and N more the rest of the latest twenty. A question counts as answered by the first note sent while it waited (one the agent decided itself is not listed). With nothing asked and nothing answered the card is not shown.

    The two are cards of the Overview, last by default: move or hide each under Account › Overview layout as any other (a layout saved before they existed gets them at its end; one that had the older single note card, or Latest notes, shows the two cards in its place). The question's line above the tabs, and the bell, lead to Agent asks; with that card hidden, the line's Answer opens the question in a dialog, answered there.

    Chat, in the top bar, is the same kind of conversation bound to no loop: it may read all your loops and keep tools and notes in your workbench (Chat). Its rail also lists your loops under Loops: go into one to open and continue its conversations there (Loop chats).

    Everywhere¶

    • Top bar: Chat · Loops · Groups · Admin (admins only). Groups shows your groups, their members and projects, and the global projects (Groups). These pages, and your Account, start with their content, not a title that repeats the bar: New loop (a plus and Loop) is at the end of the Loops list's search bar (or in the empty list), Admin's tabs and Groups' tabs are at the top.
    • Theme: system, light or dark, from the top bar; remembered in this browser.
    • Your name: at the bar's end, a button with a person drawn, before Log out; it opens your Account. A long name is cut short (its whole in the tooltip); while an admin views as a user, it is that user's name, tinted in the warning's colour above the Viewing as banner.
    • Bell: a run ends or fails, an agent asks a question, a loop is shared with you.
    • The browser tab names what is open and what it is, never the sub-tab: a loop "cpu_tune — Loop" on every tab of it, a chat "CPU block diagram — Chat" (no chat open: "Chat"), a project "RISC-V — hw-team" (its group after it), a group "hw-team — Group", Admin's page "Users — Admin", the list "Loops", your "Account". A long name is cut by the browser. A dot on the tab's icon says the most urgent thing about what is open: pink when something waits for you (the loop's agent asks you, a chat's answer asks, a change it proposes waits for Accept), else red when the loop failed, else blue while the loop runs or the chat answers; no dot when idle. On the Loops list, Groups, Admin and your Account the dot is pink while a loop of yours or one of your chats asks you, and absent otherwise.
    • Pages update by themselves. What a page shows is read again when the server says it changed: a pass ends, a design is measured, a note, an answer or a file comes, an agent drafting a loop writes a line, asks or writes a file, someone saves the problem or a setting. Only the part that changed is read again; the cards and lists stay where they are, with your scroll, open folds and selection. Nothing is asked while nothing changes. Something you are editing (the configurator, Direct edit) is never replaced: a line says Changed on the server with Reload. A tab left alone pauses its updates; come back, or move the mouse over it, and it catches up at once with what changed meanwhile.
    • Long lists come a page at a time. The audit, Maintenance's History, a loop's agent turns, its ideas, a folder of many files and Live's past starts show at once the rows that fill the window (and half as many again); scrolling to the list's end brings the next ones, twice as many each time, a spinner while they come and "N of M" while more are left. A list that fits comes whole. New rows (an audit line, a turn, a task run) join at the top while you look, and the rows you scrolled to stay.
    • Raw and Fullscreen on files, logs, results and agent turns.
    • Addresses name the view, so a link opens it: #/app/NAME/live/timeline, #/app/NAME/settings/problem?step=graph.
    • On a phone the account items move to the menu button (three lines), and lists and tabs stack; on a narrow one the bar's links draw smaller so all four fit beside the bell and the menu.

    The Preview app runs the same pages in your browser on sample data.
