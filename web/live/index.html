    Live¶

    A loop's Live tab shows its run task by task as it happens, and replays earlier starts. Two views: Graph (default) and Timeline (live/timeline). Old live/tree and live/log addresses open the Graph; the start's log is in Files, and on the Overview's Log when a run stopped on an error.

    The toolbar¶

    • Start: which start to show: the running one or an older one (latest 50; Earlier for more).
    • Follow: keep the latest work in view. Scrolling, zooming or selecting stops it; press again to resume.
    • The head: Tasks, the start's tasks and their counts (done, failed, retried). The loop's passes are the Overview's Passes, counted from its record.
    • With pipelining off and three passes or more, a hint may say "Pipelining could make passes about 24% shorter: Settings › Problem › Extra › Pipelining." (shown at 10% or more).

    Graph¶

    One pass as the loop's boxes: Search, Design, Check, Measure, Choose, and any critic.

    • Each box says its setting, ✓ or ✗, and ×N when it ran again. Skipped boxes are dashed.
    • A design of several tries has a bar per try: ✓, ! (not built) or ✗.
    • Pass chain above: a bubble per pass, coloured by outcome (Pass 4 shown). Click one to draw it. … expands every pass; Less folds them.
    • Ideas: a pass testing a queued idea skips search; Search says queued idea, and the inspector links to Results › Ideas. A design that takes a suggested idea says suggested idea.
    • Check and Measure have a bar per step (check or stage), not per run. The tooltip sums its runs (check unit: 9 of 11 passed, 1 failed, 1 running); a click opens the latest. A stage not reached yet is grey (measurement perf: waiting), hollow once the pass is over (not reached).
    • Design opens the agent's draft and conversation. Its Rationale lists the key changes and why.
    • Agents go by the names the document gives them (codeagent, defined under agents:); the tooltip adds the kind it runs as (codeagent (Claude Code)). A Search the document gives an agent says who picks, as VS Code's graph does (codeagent · explore). Its agent picks from the second pass on: a first draft follows the search policy, and Search says so.
    • Many designs at once (a re-check, several per pass): one row per design, a dot per check or stage (✓ #214 ●●●●, ✓ #246 ●● 24m 07s). Dots: green done, red failed, blue running, grey waiting, hollow never reached. The latest five show ("34 earlier designs" folded). Click a row for its checks or stages, then each for its command, exit and output.
    • A stage that exits non-zero is a failed measurement (red dot, ✗).
    • Standing design measured again: after a pass, the standing design is measured on the same stage to compare fairly. If it had to be re-measured, its row has an amber tag: tools changed, inputs changed, command changed, readout changed, vars changed, last try failed or not cached, with details (yosys rebuilt, bench.py edited). Old numbers are kept.

    Timeline¶

    The start over time in three rows. Long starts scroll sideways.

    Row Shows
    Setup Each pass's Setup (grey: Setup steps), the start's steps (opening the record, a reused baseline, the re-check queue, closing the record), a Choose that ran without a tool slot, the background tasks, and a rest on an endpoint that stayed down (a faint dotted line). One line at every zoom: steps at once overlap, the shorter drawn on top
    Design A line per lane (a trunk's: a line per trunk). A pass stays on its lane: Search (yellow), then a bar per try (blue), then the design's tag (#42), all one box. A turn waiting for its endpoint is hatched where its work would be. With pipeline, a repair that waited for its lane is a dashed box on the same lane. A lane held by a re-check (R5.5) is a faint dotted line
    Tool A line per tool slot. A design's Check, each Measure stage and Choose in one box on the slot they held, the design's tag at the box's right end; dashed while running. Agent probes are hatched, outlined in pink. A step's wait for room on the machine is a hatched grey block inside its box
    • Hover a design anywhere to light all its parts.
    • One pass at a time: passes are alternating columns, numbered at the top.
    • A bar is work, not waiting. A step's wait is a hatched grey block where it waited: for room on this machine's pool (cores, memory) inside its box on the Tool row -- the box begins at the step's ask, and a design whose first step still waits is a box holding the block alone -- or, for a tool slot, on its design's own Design line. Hover it: "Check: waited 27s for room on this machine (needs 1 core, 4 GB; free 0 cores, 1 GB)", "Measure · screen: waited 1m 10s for a tool slot", "so far" while it waits. An agent's own probe (flux loop gate|measure) waiting is drawn the same, from the moment it asked. An ideas cleanup waiting for a turn between its requests is a faint dotted line on its line. A turn waiting for its endpoint (down or busy: no try spent, no slot held) is hatched on its line, its tooltip naming the endpoint, how long and the tries since it last answered; after 30 minutes the run rests, a dotted line on Setup until it answers (When an agent's endpoint fails).
    • Designs decide in the order their measurements end. A trunk design that waited for earlier ones keeps its tool slot, and a faint dotted line in its box before its Choose says so on hover: "waited 1m 10s to decide after #3898 (2 ahead)". A flow design waits only while another design's decision is being made, and keeps its slot the same way.
    • A pink strip along a bar's foot marks where an agent or FluxAgent worked.
    • A repaired design is one box with its tag (#4), whichever try passed. A re-check or a draft you Evaluate runs as an R unit, numbered after the pass before it: R5.5 in the chain, the rows and the tables. Its designs say re-checked or handed in, evaluated.
    • Stop now marks the steps it cut interrupted (amber), never failed. The next start first measures what was cut: a bar per design, catch-up of a stopped run.
    • The head says what runs: pass 3, or passes 5–8 while several run at once.
    • A row shows at most six lines ("+3" hidden). The unroll tool, or clicking "+3", shows all; this browser remembers.
    • Click a bar to open it; Tab and Enter step through bars. A Setup bar opens its whole Setup (a pass's, the start's, a bud's, or Background in a run from before Nov 18) as the Graph's box lists it, the clicked step picked; click a step there to open it alone. Other work drawn on the Setup row (a Choose, a Plan, a Search bud's turn) opens itself.
    • Zoom out, Zoom in, Fit to the window; Ctrl+wheel (or pinch) zooms at the pointer; double-click zooms to a bar. Zoom is kept per loop until the tab closes.
    • A Setup step under half a second is a note, not a bar. Per box: calls, average, longest, total, share of the wall clock.

    A trunk-mode loop¶

    A trunk loop shows trunks and branches instead of passes.

    Graph:

    • A Design box per agent (Design · claude · t1): state (writing, queue full, waiting, stopped), turn, time, designs handed in.
    • A bar per hand-in: box colour when measured, red failed Check, amber error, faint pending. The latest 10 show; +137 counts earlier ones.
    • Click the box for the agent's latest turn.
    • 3 running · 2 waiting of 6, then a chain: Setup (S), a chip per design (#42), running ones with a blue dot, waiting ones dashed. A Setup run again is a chip (S3).
    • Pick a chip to draw its branch (Check, Measure, Choose). Live opens on the latest running branch.
    • Run setup (beside Stop) runs Setup again, document unchanged; its ideas cleanup ignores the thresholds. Greyed while one runs; audited.
    • Buds run one at a time, Setup and Search in turn, the oldest due first.
    • A failed ideas cleanup shows a warning: "Ideas cleanup due (370 open, 310 new or changed) but not done", why, and when it retries. Fix the agent or pick another under Knowledge › Ideas.
    • An ideas cleanup goes on, round after round, while it merges or marks ideas. A Search bud that comes due meanwhile runs between two rounds; the cleanup goes on after it.

    Timeline: the same rows and tools.

    • Setup: start steps, then each bud: a Setup bud's steps a bar each (papers, workbench, ideas), a Search bud's Setup steps and one bar for its Search. An ideas cleanup is one bar however many rounds its agent takes (they are listed when you click it). Hover for the name (Decision, S3, Q2) and who answered; click for the bud's steps, the clicked one picked.
    • Design: a line per trunk, a bar per turn (one design), its tag at the end (#47). Each turn has its transcript (Design · t3 · turn 4 · #47).
    • Tool: each design's Check, Measure stages and decision in one box; a failed Check ends red. The start's re-check sits under a grey band, R · re-check. A draft you Evaluate while a flow's passes run has its box here too, beside theirs, tagged with its design.

    Counts on a trunk's Live are the picked start's only.

    The inspector¶

    Select a task to open it.

    • Tool task: command, folder, exit, time, output (live).
    • Agent task: reply, then the conversation (text, thinking, tool calls with inputs and outputs).
    • Model try: tools called, then the reply in parts (why, idea, code, diffs), tokens.
    • Raw and Fullscreen show everything.

    Internals permission (Admin › People › Users): only people with it see prompts, thinking and each action. Everyone else (the owner included) sees status, final reply, the search direction without its reason, and a count line: 14 tool calls · 3 probes · 12m 30s. This applies to every agent. Your own stage and check commands stay visible.

    A turn starts with a row of pills: agent, model, status, exit 0, 37 turns, tokens, cost, thinking on, Tool calls (12). Hover for the label (tokens: 2.0M in · 19.0k out). The Agents tab and Chat show the same pills.

    • A task shows its path: Pass 2 › Design › try 8, … › try 8 › call 6.
    • An improve says its starting stage (from the esl stage).
    • A failed or unbuilt try says why.
    • A box lists its runs; a box still to come shows its setting (YAML folded).
    • A pass shows what it measured last and whether it was kept, where its time went, and each box ("Design · 3 tries · 2 not built"). A box never reached says "not run".
    • A check's failures read "14 of 18 fail".

    Choose names its pick and pool, and how long it waited for earlier designs to decide. Its box says the objectives, each with its way ("cycle ↓ · inst +3"); a tracked one has no arrow and is never a best.

    • A design a critic rejected is struck through with a critic tag; the reason is below. The tag, Open and Choose · critique · #N -- rejected open the critic's turn.
    • A dictionary metric shows one number: its objective's aggregate, else its _a, else the mean. Hover for entries.
    • Designs not in the pool are muted with why: "screen only", "cut on screen", "stale", "same design as #137", "rejected by the critic", "the baseline", a removed stage, or why not recorded.
    • If one beats the pick, a notice names it: "3 designs have a smaller jx_time than #131 and were not in the pool: #140 (377 ns) was measured on screen only: its pass ended before it climbed to full. ..."
