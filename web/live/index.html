    Live¶

    A loop's Live tab shows what its run does, task by task, as it happens, and what earlier starts did. It has two views, Graph and Timeline, and always shows every pass of the start: the Graph picks the pass it draws from its chain of passes, the Timeline draws them all side by side. It opens on the Graph; live/timeline opens the Timeline. An address of the Tree or the Log, views Live had before (live/tree, live/log), opens the Graph; the start's log is the Overview's Log when a run stopped on an error, and Files.

    The toolbar¶

    • Start picks the start to show: the one running, or an older one, which replays its passes, tools and conversations. It lists the latest 50 starts; Earlier at its end brings the ones before them.
    • Follow keeps the latest work in view as the run goes on, in the Graph and the Timeline alike: the Timeline at its end (now), at any zoom, the latest task's bar in sight. Pressed while it follows (its tooltip says which); scrolling the view, zooming or selecting a task stops it and leaves the view where you put it; Follow again resumes, back at the latest work at once.

    The head over the view is one line: All passes, then its counts -- its tasks (grey), done, failed, retried -- each count's word in its tooltip. When pipelining is off and the loop's record holds three passes or more, a quiet line under it may say turning it on is worth it: "Pipelining could make passes about 24% shorter: Settings › Problem › Extra › Pipelining." -- worked out from the record with nothing run (the time from each pass's design passing Check to its decision, against the whole pass), and said only when it is 10% or more. With pipelining on, nothing is said. flux loop passes ends with the same advice (budget.pipeline: true).

    Graph¶

    The pass as the loop's drawing: Search, Design, Check, Measure, Choose, and a critic where one is set. Each box says what it is set to (Search what it picked), whether it passed (✓ or ✗), and ×N when it ran again; a design of several tries has a bar per try, ✓, ! (not built) or ✗. Boxes the loop skipped are dashed. A pass that tests an idea queued in Results › Ideas runs no search: the queued idea is its direction. Its Search box says queued idea (the queue's icon) instead of skipped, as do the tree and the pass's flow; its Design carries the same tag, the idea it follows in its tooltip, and the Search's inspector names the idea with a link to Results › Ideas. With nothing queued, a pass may suggest the best-scored idea not measured yet; when the design takes it, the Design box says suggested idea, the idea in its tooltip. Above the flow is the chain of passes, one bubble a pass in the colour of how it came out, and which pass is drawn (Pass 4 shown); a bubble draws its pass and opens it in the inspector. The chain shows the start's Setup, the pass before the one drawn, it, the one after and the latest; a … between them opens every pass of the chain, Less folds it again. A pass that is over is read, not followed; Follow comes back to the work at hand. A step bar fills once its step is done; one at work pulses, light. A Check or Measure box has a bar per step, not per run: five checks run three times are five bars, one stage measured ten times is one; a bar's tooltip sums its runs (check unit: 9 of 11 passed, 1 failed, 1 running), amber when it failed on the way, and a click opens its latest run. Measure has a bar for each stage the document sets, in its order (area, perf): a stage no run has reached yet is a grey bar while the box is at work (measurement perf: waiting), hollow once it is over (not reached), as Check's checks to come. A box of one task opens it; a Design of one try opens its draft, the agent's conversation, while it drafts, while its check runs and once it is over; one of several tries opens its list. A design agent's draft, and the Design box, show the rundown it gave of the design it handed in (Rationale, a few points: its key points, what it changed and why), once its short follow-up has answered.

    A pass that checks and measures each design apart -- the re-check measuring designs again, several at once -- lists them a design at a time. Check and Measure count designs (×246, its tooltip 246 designs measured), not stretches of work. Check has a row per design with a dot per check (✓ #214 ●●●●) and Measure a row per design with a dot per stage (✓ #246 ●● 24m 07s, the design's measuring time). A dot is green when done, red when failed, blue at work, grey while waiting and hollow when never reached; its tooltip names the step, its state and its time. A stage whose command exited non-zero is a failed measurement: its dot is red, its design's row and its task are ✗, and a box whose last run it was is ✗ too (a journal from before Flux read it so is shown the same way). The latest five show, the earlier ones folded on top ("34 earlier designs"). A design's check opens its checks, a design's Measure row its stages (Measure · #246), and each check or stage opens its own detail: its command, exit and output, with ‹ Check · #210 or ‹ Measure · #246 to go back.

    After a pass's design is measured, the loop has the standing design's numbers on the same stage, so the two compare under one setup. When the standing design had to be measured again, Measure lists it as a row of its own (✓ #68 ● and ✓ #42 ●) with an amber tag naming why: tools changed, inputs changed, command changed, readout changed (how the stage reads its numbers), params changed, last try failed or not cached. Its tooltip and its panel say it in full (standing design, measured again: its stage's tools changed since 2 Nov), with what changed (yosys rebuilt, bench.py edited); its numbers from before are kept, these added beside them.

    Timeline¶

    The start's time in three rows, each named once: Setup, Design and Tool. It takes the page's width, the inspector under it; a long start scrolls sideways, the row names kept in view, and only the part in sight (and a view's width either side) is drawn.

    • Setup: the start's own steps -- opening the record, a baseline reused, the papers, the workbench -- each in its box's colour, on as few lines as steps at work at once need; the decisions that are over many designs: the baseline's, the re-check's, the closing decision; and a design's Choose that ran without a tool slot (below).
    • Design: a line per design at work at once, budget.designs_at_once lines. A pass is one design's work, in one box on its line: its Search (yellow) first, then each try its own bar (blue), and at the hand-in the design's tag (#42). The time between two tries draws nothing.
    • Tool: budget.tools_at_once lines, whether pipeline is on or off. A design's tool work is one box: its Check, a bar per Measure stage, then its Choose, in the boxes' colours, the design's tag (#42) at the end of its Measure part where there is room. While it runs the box is dashed to now. A pass whose earlier pass has not decided yet waits for its turn before its Choose, its tool slot given back meanwhile: its box ends at its last Measure stage, and its Choose, run without a slot, is an orange bar on the Setup row, its tooltip naming the design. So the Tool row never needs more lines than slots. A search pass's checks and measurements are bars of their own (its designs are checked and measured together). A check or measurement an agent ran itself in its try (a probe) takes a Tool line too, hatched in its kind's colour and outlined in the agent's pink; a try's check that sent the design back is a short outlined bar there.

    Hovering a design -- its box on the Design line, its tag, its box on the Tool line or its Choose on Setup -- lights them all, so you can follow a design from its Search to its decision. One pass at a time, each pass is a column behind the rows, white and light grey by turns, its number at the top; passes at work at once have no columns, the Design lines say them.

    A bar is a step's work, not its wait. A step waiting for a tool slot (or for room on the machine) draws nothing until its tool starts, so the Tool row never draws more boxes at once than there are slots (tools_at_once: 8: eight tools at once, a design's check, measurements and decision holding one). How long it waited is said in its task ("waited 3m for a tool slot"), and in the box's tooltip when a step waited inside it (a journal of an older version, when checks and measurements had slots of their own). Lines are filled by time: a line a design freed is the next design's, and a bar never moves to another line as the run goes on.

    A row draws at most six lines; the work on the lines past them is not drawn (the row's name says "+3": the lines hidden). The unroll tool (two bars, arrows spreading), in the header when a row is that wide, draws every line instead; pressed again, it folds back. Clicking a row's "+3" does the same. This browser remembers the choice. A task picked elsewhere (the Graph, a link, the arrow keys) whose bar is on a hidden line unrolls the rows; while Following, the row's "+3" is marked instead.

    Every bar is its own: a click selects that one step and opens it in the inspector, and Tab and Enter reach the bars in time order. A bar too short to see is still clicked a few pixels either side of it. Beside the unroll tool, Zoom out, Zoom in and Fit to the window widen the time axis (twice as wide each click) and bring it back to the page's width; the chart then scrolls sideways inside its box (by touch on a phone), the time axis's ticks spaced to round steps. Zooming keeps the moment in the middle of the view where it is, or the end of a running start when it was in view, so the chart keeps showing what runs now. Ctrl and the mouse wheel (a trackpad's pinch) zoom around the pointer; double-clicking a bar zooms in until it fills half the view. The zoom is kept for each loop until the browser tab is closed, apart from the unroll tool's choice. Where an agent or FluxAgent works a step (a try's draft, a Setup step, a Search), a pink strip along the foot of its bar says when; the strip is always inside its own bar, at any zoom. The Setup row holds what the start reads and writes before its work: opening the record (from the moment its file is opened: on a first start its tables are made then), a baseline reused (baseline: its decisions read back), and before a re-check's first Check its row on the record (re-check on record) and its queue (re-check queue: the designs to measure again and the measured ones read back); and at a pass's end its connection to the record closing (close record: the record's log written back into it, slow on a slow disk). Each Setup step is a bar when it takes half a second or more, its tooltip saying which part took the time, and a note in its box otherwise. Per box: how many calls, the average, the longest, the total, and its share of the wall clock.

    A trunk-mode loop¶

    A loop run as a trunk has a Live that shows its trunks and its branches instead of passes.

    The Graph starts with the trunk: a Design box per agent (Design · claude · t1), at work for the whole run. Its line says what it does now -- writing, queue full (its share of the queue is waiting), waiting (between turns) or stopped -- then its turn, how long it has worked and how many designs it handed in; a bar for each of them, in the box's colour when measured, red when it failed its Check, amber on an error, faint while it waits or runs, and one more pulsing while the agent writes. Only the 10 latest are drawn: after a long run +137 starts the row, the earlier hand-ins counted (1k past a thousand; its tooltip says 137 earlier hand-ins). Pressing the box opens the agent's latest turn. Under it, 3 running · 2 waiting of 6, then the chain: the start's Setup (S) and a chip per design (#42), the running ones with a blue dot, a failed one red, those waiting dashed at the end. Picking a chip draws that design's branch as a pass's boxes -- Check, Measure, Choose -- under a line saying the design, what it came from, its agent and why; pressing a box opens its task. Live opens on the latest running branch. A Setup run again during the trunk is a chip of its own (S3) where it began among the designs.

    However many designs there are, the chain is one line, as the pass chain: the first chip, …, the chip before the one picked, it, the one after, …, the last (a waiting design while some wait). Picking a chip moves that window onto it; a … opens the chips it hides, in its place (the line scrolls sideways when they do not fit), and Less folds them again.

    Run setup, beside Stop in the head of a running trunk loop's Live, runs the Setup again (when the library changed, say), the document unchanged. It is greyed out while one runs or is asked; who pressed it is in the audit log.

    The Timeline is the one passes have -- the same zoom, unroll tool, selection and rows: Setup, Design and Tool. Setup: the start's own steps in their boxes' colours -- the re-check's and the closing decision's choice among them -- then a bar for each Setup run again (a setup or Search bud). Every step is in its box's colour as in a flow: a Search bud's own Setup steps (tools ready, the record opened) are grey bars before its Search bar, and that bar opens the bud's Search turn; hovering it names who answered (FluxAgent and its model, or the agent) and the tools it read the record with. No words are written on these bars: hovering one names it (Decision, S3, Q2). Design: a line per trunk, named at the left (t1, t2), a bar per turn -- a turn is one design -- from when the trunk began readying the turn (its brief) to its end, a pink strip on it where its agent works, and at its end the tag of the design it handed in (#47). Each turn has its own transcript in the inspector, titled with the design it handed in (*Design · t3 · turn 4 ·

    47*); a turn that handed none in says how many in a row. Tool: budget.tools_at_once lines; each design's Check, a¶

    bar per Measure stage and its decision in one box, its tag at the end of its Measure part where there is room; a failed Check ends its box with a red mark. Hovering a design -- its turn, a tag or its box -- lights its turn, its tags and its box; each bar -- a turn, a step, a stage -- opens its own task. The re-check at the start has no turn: its designs' boxes sit under a grey band, R · re-check, each with its tag. The checks an agent ran itself in its turn (flux loop gate, flux loop measure) take Tool lines too, hatched in their kind's colour and outlined in pink: probes, counted with the tasks, never the loop's measurements. Past six lines the rest are hidden (+N: the lines hidden); the unroll tool or the +N draws every line.

    Every count on a trunk's Live is the start picked in Start: a trunk box's handed in, running, waiting, the chain and the Timeline count what that start did, not what earlier starts left on the record (a design still waiting from before is in the queue this start takes from).

    The inspector¶

    Selecting a task opens it beside the list. A tool's task shows its command, folder, exit and time, and its output as it runs. An agent's task shows its reply, then its conversation: text, thinking and tool calls, each input and output. A model's try reads the same way: the tools it called, then its reply in parts (why, the idea, the design as code, each edit as a diff) and its tokens. Raw and Fullscreen open the whole of it.

    Only people with the Internals permission (Admin › People › Users) see the prompts the loop's agents and models were given, their thinking, and each of their actions: every tool call, command, file read or written, search and result. Everyone else, the loop's owner included, sees the turn's status, its final reply, a pass's search direction without its reason, and its actions as counts and time: 14 tool calls · 3 probes · 12m 30s, one line where the calls would be. This holds for every agent: the loop's own, Chat's and the New-loop author. The commands of your own stages and checks are your configuration, not an agent's actions, so they stay visible.

    An agent's or a model's turn starts with its facts as one row of small pills, each with its icon: the agent (in the agent's pink), the model, its status, exit 0, 37 turns, its tokens in and out, its cost, thinking on, Tool calls (12). Hover a pill (or read it with a screen reader) for its label: tokens: 2.0M in · 19.0k out. An exit other than 0 and an error take their colour; the turn's state stays in the pill beside its title. The Agents tab and Chat's Thinking & commands show a turn's facts the same way.

    Under its title a task says where it is, the whole path: Pass 2 › Design › try 8, a tool call … › try 8 › call 6. An improve says the stage it starts from (from the esl stage), and its steps are rows that open them (its draft). A try that failed or was not built says why in a line under that. A box shows its runs with their state and time, its total time in its head; a box still to come says what the document sets it to, in a few words, with the YAML folded under them. A pass shows what it measured last and whether that was kept, where its time went (in the bar's order) and its six boxes, each with how many times it worked ("Design · 3 tries · 2 not built"); once it is over, a box it never reached says "not run", and a failed pass says why. A check's failures read "14 of 18 fail".

    Choose's panel names its pick and its pool. A design a critic rejected is struck through in its table with a critic tag, and the critic's reason is said under the table; the tag, the reason's Open and the step Choose · critique · #N -- rejected all open the critic's turn. Only this Choose's critiques strike its rows, not another pass's. A dictionary metric is one number there, as the loop ranks it: its objective's aggregate, else the dictionary's own _a, else the mean; hover a cell for its entries. The rows sort by it, and "same numbers as" compares it.

    The table also lists the designs measured by then that were not in the pool, muted, each with why in a few words: "screen only" (its pass ended, or the run stopped, before it climbed to the stage the choice is made on), "cut on screen" (a cutoff), "stale" (measured before a change to how its stage is measured), "same design as #137", "rejected by the critic", "the baseline", a stage the document no longer has. Hover a row for the whole reason; when nothing recorded says why, the row says why not recorded rather than guess. When such a design beats the pick on the first column's objective, a notice over the table names each one with its value and why it was not compared: "3 designs have a smaller jx_time than #131 and were not in the pool: #140 (377 ns) was measured on screen only: its pass ended before it climbed to full. ..."
