    Results¶

    A loop's Results tab holds every design measured across all starts. The decision is on one line above three views: Designs, Graphs, Ideas. Library digests are under Files › Library.

    Designs¶

    Five tabs, each with a count. Results opens on Accepted -- on All while no design is accepted yet -- and then on the tab you picked last; the address names the tab (…/results?tab=failed) and the open design (…/results?design=NAME).

    Tab Holds
    All Every design handed in: accepted, rejected, failed and pending (the Graphs draw the accepted and rejected, whose numbers count)
    Accepted Checked and measured; every limit met at the judging stage
    Rejected Measured but missing a limit or cutoff, or rejected by a person or critic ("rejected by bob", "rejected by the critic (final)" / "(this pick)"). Never drawn on graphs. Also designs stopped at screen (cut early; their numbers muted)
    Failed A check refused it, or a measurement gave nothing. Shows its Checks pill (e.g. ✗ gate), the reason and when. Drafts cut by a stop end the list as "stopped before handed in" (no design, not counted)
    Pending Checked but not yet measured through, or re-measure pending (old numbers muted)
    • Checks pill (Failed, Pending): the deepest stage reached and its latest outcome (✓ gate, ✗ esl). Hover for every stage.
    • Latest attempt wins: if a newer attempt fails at a stage, older results past it are superseded: the design moves to Failed and its old numbers stop counting everywhere. Old attempts stay in its detail, folded.
    • A pass that tried to improve a design but made no new one ("no design") leaves the design's numbers alone.
    • Measured before a change (to the stage's command, readout, vars, input files or tools; When a measurement still counts): muted with a mark; not counted. The Overview's card offers Accept as current, Leave as stale, Re-measure.
    • Columns are the metrics the current attempts measured and the document names. A metric only old attempts carry leaves the lists once anything is measured again.
    • The table loads a page at a time ("32 of 300"); rows do not reshuffle while a loop runs.

    The table's tools¶

    Icons at the end of the tab row:

    Icon Does
    Star The loop's ranking (default order): decision, then rank, then objective. Click a header to sort; a third click (or the star) returns to the ranking. Empty values sort last
    % Relative: percent change from the latest baseline (else the accepted designs' P90, P10 when minimized, the median for an unranked metric). Hover for value and reference
    Eye Show hidden columns (Settings › Preferences), dimmed
    { } Inspect one dictionary: its entries become the columns. ✕ or None returns
    Two columns Compare two ticked designs: every number at every stage, the change (green better, red worse), the source diff
    • A number from an earlier stage is muted; hover says which ("· from timing").
    • A dictionary metric's column is its aggregate (its objective's, else _a, else the mean). Tick an entry in Settings › Preferences (▸) to add it as a column. Missing entries are never zero. {} raw shows every number.

    A design's detail¶

    • What it misses, its numbers per stage, its idea.
    • Rationale: the design agent's own summary of its changes and why (none for a model step's design).
    • Its checks, one row per stage: the latest attempt (outcome, time, output); earlier attempts folded (2 earlier attempts); superseded stages muted.
    • Its source, notes pinned from Chat, and Reference for comparison if marked (for reading only; not the baseline).
    • Its pass opens in Live.

    Checked, not measured¶

    A design only checks judged shows Not measured and why (the refusing check, "its screen measurement was refused: estimated to fail", or what it waits for). Check and measure again (owner, with a reason) queues it in Pending: the running loop does it before its next pass; a loop at rest runs it now.

    Probed by agents¶

    Drafts an agent probed but did not hand in end the Rejected and Failed tables under probed by agents (3), folded.

    • Named by pass and try (#p4.2; #pt1.1 under trunk t1). The agent's numbers are muted.
    • An unverified pill; hover for why ("not verified by the loop — probed by the agent only").
    • Never in graphs, the decision or standings.
    • Evaluate (owner or editor; audited) has the loop check, measure and choose it as a design: in a running flow with designs at once, at its next free lane; else at the next re-check.
    • It also leaves the list once a design with exactly its text passes Check.

    Reject a design¶

    • Reject (owner, with a reason): kept and listed in Rejected with why and by whom, never chosen. Tick its numbers to re-measure or clear them.
    • A critic's rejection names the critic and what it rejected (the design, final; or this pick, which lapses when the numbers change), with Show the critic's reasoning.
    • Restore undoes every rejection on it (a person's, a critic's, a refused re-measure).
    • Check and measure again appears beside Restore after a refused re-measure. The detail says when it runs.
    • Designs hidden by hand before Nov 2026 read as rejections.

    Who made a design¶

    With several makers (trunks, or several Design agents), each design has a coloured tag after its number (t2, or the agent). Hover: "made by trunk t1 (claude, opus)". One maker: no tags.

    Star your key designs¶

    Star (first tool in a design's head) bookmarks it. Owner and editors star; everyone who can read the loop sees stars. Each star is audited with who and when.

    • The decision has an orange trophy; a starred design an open orange star.
    • Results › Graphs draw starred points in orange (Starred in the legend). The Overview does not.
    • Stars change nothing the loop does.

    Share a design¶

    Share (after Star; needs the Share right) makes a read-only link to one design: for people signed in, or anyone with the link, for 1 to 90 days (never expires: signed-in only). The dialog lists links with Copy and Revoke; Account › Shared links lists all.

    The page is live and shows:

    • the design's and loop's short names, standing, trophy;
    • Measurements per stage;
    • Among the designs: on the Pareto front or how many beat it, change against the baseline; other designs only as a grey density (no numbers);
    • its idea, rationale and Checks outcomes.

    Options: Include the design file (text up to 512 KB, with Download), then and its diff with the baseline (changed lines with three lines of context).

    Never shared: code (unless included), check output, notes, rejection reasons, other designs' names, the loop, the library, hidden files. A revoked, expired or broken link shows This link does not open (Share).

    Invalidate, re-evaluate¶

    After a check or measurement script changed, use Settings › Record:

    • Invalidate all (this loop, at rest, confirmed): sets every design aside in Pending as re-measure pending, never chosen ("40 of 40 set aside since …: re-measure pending until a re-evaluation runs"). Running it again adds only newer designs.
    • Re-Evaluate: the next start, after its baseline, re-checks and re-measures the designs of the tabs you tick ("Rejected · 143"), then runs its passes. The dialog says what it will do ("Re-checks and re-measures 12 accepted and 143 rejected designs at the next start, after its baseline").
      • Accepted, Rejected, Failed, Pending: that tab's designs now. Pending is ticked by default; Invalidate all's designs are measured only with it. All four: every design. A hand rejection stands after re-measuring.
      • Rerun baseline: pass 0 first.
      • Designs already re-measure pending are measured anyway.
      • Cancel until the start. Progress: "re-measured 12 of 40 since the invalidation at …".
      • A stop keeps what was measured ("paused: 12 of 40 done; continues at the next start").
      • A design today's checks refuse goes to Failed.

    Known results are reused. A design that comes back with the same text is refused (or passed) from the record without running again: "refused here before as #N, not run again" / "passed here before as #N, not run again". An edit to the document or a file the check reads, or Re-Evaluate, runs it again.

    Graphs¶

    Graph Shows
    Pareto front Any two metrics at the deciding stage (or a stage you pick). The pool drawn; other designs faint as excluded, each saying why ("measured on screen only: its pass ended before it climbed to full"). Decision ringed, front joined
    Improvement by design Best so far per metric, design by design, titled "best cycles (-12.9%)". Baselines are grey lines. Charts share one x axis

    Each graph's chips:

    Chip Does
    Stage: deepest ▾ Which stage's numbers to draw
    Accepted only Draw only Accepted designs
    Focus Fade every point off the front (Pareto) or that set no new best
    Zoom to an area Drag a box to zoom (flat drag: x only; upright: y only). Right-click goes back, double-click shows all, Escape cancels
    ⛶ Fullscreen
    • Accepted only, Focus and Zoom apply to all the loop's graphs and the Overview's, kept in your browser. Metrics, axes and stage are saved with the loop.
    • Selected design: click a point or open a row; it is an orange diamond on every graph and an orange edge in the table. Click again or Escape to clear.
    • Which way is better comes only from an objective's direction or goal (>= 300, <= 0.5). A metric no objective names is drawn without a best line ("not an objective") and its changes are not coloured.
    • A design stopped at screen is drawn hollow.
    • Old loops with parts (from before one loop was one design): part designs are named by part (front: #3), tinted by part with a key, and never in the decision's pool.

    Ideas¶

    The loop's ideas notebook: each idea and the designs that tested it.

    State Pill Meaning
    proposed grey ring Not tested yet: no design has followed it.
    testing turning blue ring A design that follows it is being checked or measured now.
    checked green ring A design that follows it passed its checks but has no numbers yet.
    measured green check A design that follows it has numbers (its hover: "measured, but no sign it helped"); whether it helped is in its evidence and its score.
    refused red cross Every design that tested it was refused by the checks.
    interrupted orange bang Its last test stopped before an outcome (the loop was stopped).

    Evidence: one row per design that followed the idea: the furthest stage reached, the outcome there, the numbers. Earlier tries fold in ("2 earlier tries"). A design's furthest meaningful try speaks for it; earlier failures do not count against it.

    Outcome Meaning
    win Measured better than the standing design at that stage on some objective, by more than the noise, and worse on none.
    no gain Measured within the noise of the standing design on every objective.
    loss Measured worse beyond the noise, and better on none.
    tests refused A check ran and refused the design: failed tests, or a critic. This counts against the idea.
    checked Passed a check with no numbers yet, or a prototype that passes every input and misses the cost goal.
    inconclusive Says nothing about the idea: it did not build, the check did not run, the coding agent or a tool failed (a quota, a time limit, a permission), or the run stopped.
    • Noise is the objective's tie and the spread of repeated measurements. "inconclusive ×4" counts those.
    • Score ("78.7 · repeated win"; hover for parts) orders the list. Wins raise it, losses and refusals lower it, untested ideas slowly age. Kinds, best first: repeated win, single win, untested with a priority, checked, inconclusive only, untested, no gain, stale, near a refused idea, loss, refused by tests. Sort by Newest or Number instead in the State column.
    • proposed again ×3 (trunk Search buds): shown only; changes nothing.
    • Filter bar: All or one state, with counts; Marked and Deleted once used; Queued last.

    Queue¶

    • The queue button on a row toggles it. Tags: next pass, queued · 2, ...
    • Each pass builds a design for the oldest queued idea until one has a meaningful outcome.
    • After three passes of only inconclusive designs it leaves the queue (Live says so): fix the setup and queue it again.
    • Queue N queues every untested idea, best first. Clear empties the queue.
    • A running loop reads the queue at its next pass.

    Merged, project and deleted ideas¶

    • Ideas cleanup folds merged ideas under the kept one ("merged into idea 3").
    • An idea from a linked project's knowledge has a tag "from silicon@hw-lab" (links to the project). A retired entry marks it for deletion, unless a measured design keeps it.
    • Delete (bin; owner and editors): gone from agents and the queue. Undo, or Restore under Deleted. Text and evaluations stay on record; audited.
    • A folded row (×N) deletes all its ideas (Delete N ideas); open it to delete one.
    • Marked for deletion (by an agent, with a reason and who): the loop reads it as before until a person picks Accept (deletes, with Undo) or Reject (keeps; the same agent cannot mark it again until new evidence). Marked N filters them. Chat's agent proposes deletions for you to Accept in Chat.

    Elsewhere¶
