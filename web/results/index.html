    Results¶

    A loop's Results tab holds every design measured across all its starts. The decision is said in a line above three views: Designs, Graphs and Ideas. The library's digests are under Files › Library (an old link to Results › Library leads there).

    Designs¶

    One card of the designs, in four tabs, each with its count:

    • All: the accepted and the failed, the decision first. A design only its checks judged is a row too, blank in the measurement columns.
    • Accepted: the designs measured and accepted by the limits at their deepest stage.
    • Failed: refused by a check, a measurement that gave nothing, a limit missed, or rejected. No measurement columns: each check it went through (a pill each), the failure reason, when.
    • Pending: designs waiting for a stage: checked and not measured yet ("its pass ended before a stage measured it", "not measured yet: its pass is still running", "the run stopped before a stage measured it"), measured but short of the stage that judges them (their numbers so far), and those set aside to be measured again (re-measure pending, their old numbers muted).

    Columns sort. Results opens on Accepted; the browser remembers the tab you choose; the address names it (…/results?tab=failed), and a link to a design opens it in its tab.

    • Absolute / Relative: numbers as they are, or as percent change from the latest baseline (without one, from the accepted designs' 90th or 10th percentile). Hover a cell for the value and its reference.
    • Numbers from an earlier stage: when stages measure different things (one timing, one area), a design's row shows each metric's deepest measurement; a number carried from an earlier stage is muted, and hovering it says which ("· from timing"). The design's detail writes that stage under the number.
    • Dictionary metrics: a dictionary's column is its own name; Tests ▸ unrolls its tests. A missing or empty test stays unmeasured, never zero.
    • Compare: tick two designs to see every number at every stage, the change (green where better), and their sources' difference.
    • A design opens with what it does not meet, its numbers at each stage, its idea and rationale, each check it went through (outcome, time, output; its pass opens in Live with each check's log), and its source; notes pinned on it from Chat, and Reference for comparison when it is marked so (for reading only: not the baseline the numbers compare with). The address names the open design (…/results?design=NAME): a link or a reload opens it.

    Checked, not measured¶

    A design only its checks judged opens the same detail as a measured one, with Not measured and why at its top: the check that refused it, a measurement tried that gave nothing ("its screen measurement was refused: estimated to fail"), or what it waits for. Check and measure again… (its owner, with a reason) has it checked and measured at every stage: by the running loop before its next pass, or at rest by a run of its own; it waits in Pending until then. flux loop show NAME says the same reason ("not measured: …").

    Reject a design¶

    Reject… on a design (its owner, with a reason) marks it: it stays shown but is never chosen. Tick its numbers to have them measured again or cleared; at rest, Also hide the design takes it out of the results, listed under Invalidated by hand, where Re-evaluate checks and measures those designs again in one run, and Restore counts them again. A critic's rejection reads the same way.

    Invalidate, re-evaluate, show hidden¶

    After a check or a measurement script changed, the loop's Settings › Maintenance has three tasks for the record:

    • Invalidate all sets every design on record aside: each stays listed in Pending with its old numbers, marked re-measure pending and never chosen. Nothing is measured again yet: above the table (and on the Overview's designs tile) it reads "40 of 40 set aside since …: re-measure pending until a re-evaluation runs". Running it again adds only designs recorded since.

    • Re-Evaluate asks the loop's next start: after its baseline, that start checks and measures again the designs of Pending -- those waiting for a stage (checked, not measured) and those set aside (re-measure pending, Invalidate's included) -- then its passes go on. Its dialog has three boxes, each adding to what the next start measures again:

      • Rerun baseline: pass 0, first (a baseline on record otherwise stands).
      • Rerun failed: the designs of Failed -- refused by a check, measured to nothing, missing a limit, or refused by an earlier re-evaluation's checks; not those rejected by hand or by a critic.
      • Rerun all: every design on record, invalidated first (one rejected by hand too; its rejection stands). It ticks the other two.

    Until that start the task's row and the Overview say what was asked ("the baseline measured again, then the pending designs re-evaluated") with Cancel; it can be asked while the loop runs. The pending or failed designs are written as one round when that start begins, each marked re-measure pending until measured. Each design's new numbers replace the old as it is measured: "re-measured 12 of 40 since the invalidation at …". A stop keeps every design measured so far: "paused: 12 of 40 done; continues at the next start" -- the next start measures only the designs still pending, the one a stop cut short included. A design today's checks refuse stays rejected, with why. - Show hidden evaluations is for records invalidated by an older Flux, where an invalidation hid every row up to it. The rows of designs never measured again since come back with their old numbers, set aside (re-measure pending) as Invalidate leaves them; the old rows of designs measured again since stay history (their new numbers stand), and designs invalidated by hand stay under Invalidated by hand. Its dialog says how many rows and designs it shows again. Nothing is deleted: the old mark stays on the record, beside the event that shows the rows again.

    Graphs¶

    • Pareto front: any two metrics, by default at the stage the loop decides on: the designs it decides among (its pool) drawn and counted, every other design faint at the numbers it has, its point saying why it is not in the pool ("not a finalist on screen", "same design as …", a stage the document no longer has). Pool only hides the faint ones; picking a stage draws that stage's numbers instead. Accepted and failed coloured, the decision a diamond, the front joined. Focus fits the axes to the feasible front.
    • Improvement by design: the best so far for each metric chosen, design after design. Baselines are gray reference lines.

    The choices (metrics, axes, stage, scope) are saved with the loop.

    Ideas¶

    The loop's ideas notebook: each idea a model or agent proposed, and the trials that evaluated it. "Measured" means there is evidence, not that it improved anything; "refused" means every design that tested it was refused.

    With the ideas cleanup on, an idea merged into another is folded under it ("merged into #3"), its evidence counted with the kept idea's, and the ideas it ranked come first, the most promising first, each with its reason; ideas proposed since come after them, newest first.

    Elsewhere¶

    • Overview shows the decision against the limits, the best three designs, and a best-so-far chart. Its cards are chosen under Account › Overview layout.
    • flux report RECORD writes the same results as an HTML report beside the record.
