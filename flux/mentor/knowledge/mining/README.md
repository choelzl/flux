# knowledge/mining/ — facts computed from stores, boundaries attached

`flux-knowledge-mining` ([decisions.md D243](../../../docs/decisions.md)): typed,
provenance-carrying facts computed from the campaign and calibration stores — the Knowledge role
learning from the Evaluator's own measured history rather than only from ingested spec text.
Exposed the usual three-surfaces way via `flux_mine_knowledge`.

Five fact kinds, each a `Fact` with `kind`/`statement`/`evidence`/`scope`/`not_established`/
`pointers`:

- `estimator_bias` — the observed prediction/reference ratio range per residual family;
- `measured_point` — rung measurements per campaign (never screen-phase estimates);
- `observed_ratio` — the effect of ONE knob doubling between two measured candidates;
- `refusal_pattern` — stored failure messages, grouped by the exact verbatim message;
- `frontier_outcome` — completed campaigns' frontiers with per-metric fidelity labels.

The design center is the failure mode this package must NOT have — a fact that reads as more
than the data supports. Four rules, enforced by construction rather than by care:

1. **Computed, never asserted.** Every number comes from stored rows; every statement is
   generated by fixed wording from those numbers. There is no free-text field an author (human
   or model) fills in.
2. **Measured language only.** Statements say "measured", "observed on", "refused with" —
   past-tense reports of what happened, never "is", "scales as", or "will".
3. **The boundary is part of the fact.** `scope` (exactly where the evidence lives) and
   `not_established` (the inference the numbers do NOT license) are fields the consumer
   receives, not documentation.
4. **Pointers or it didn't happen.** Every fact names the store rows it was computed from —
   campaign ids, trial seqs, calibration record ids, content hashes — so any consumer can
   re-derive it.

Deliberately not mined: fitted scaling laws (only measured point-pairs and their ratio),
screen-phase estimates presented as measurements (they appear only inside bias facts, AS the
predictions they were), caveated calibration records pooled into a range, non-done campaigns
(counted in `skipped`, never silently dropped), and normalized/clustered refusal messages.

`render_facts_for_prompt(facts, max_facts=12)` ([decisions.md D245](../../../docs/decisions.md))
is the ONE renderer feeding facts to consumers — the campaign proposers
(`flux_campaign_step(knowledge_facts=...)`) and objective authoring
(`flux_author_objective(facts=...)`) — so the wording cannot drift between them. It renders each
fact's statement together with its NOT-established boundary, never the statement alone, and
states its cap when it bites. Mined facts are deliberately NOT ingested into the BM25 corpus
index: mined measured facts and licensed spec text have different provenance classes, and mixing
them would blur exactly the boundary the `pointers` field exists to keep sharp.
