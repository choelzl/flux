    Split a larger design¶

    A design too large to write or check in one piece can be split: into parts of one artifact, or into sub-loops, each a loop of its own whose decided designs a parent composes. Use one or the other, never both. The fields are in the Document reference.

    Parts of one design¶

    parts:
  decoder: the 8b10b decoder
  encoder: the 8b10b encoder

    parts is a list of names, a map of names to descriptions, or decompose to let the orchestrator divide the goal itself (into at most 8). Names are a letter, then letters, digits, _ and -. Each part is drafted and checked on its own, then the parts are joined in order, separated by a blank line, and the whole is measured.

    Parts suit a model or agent generator that writes one file in pieces. budget.parallel_parts drafts several at once; budget.regenerate (or --regenerate PART) drafts a part again instead of reusing the admitted one.

    Sub-loops¶

    subtasks: [ops/recip, ops/exp]
flow:
  generate: {command: "{python} {home}/compose.py {parts} {artifact}"}

    Each folder in subtasks holds its own problem.yaml, named by its folder (no id). A child inherits the parent's contract, language, flow (checks, measurements, space and knowledge included), objectives, skills, params, budget and ladder unless it says them; flow, budget and params merge key by key, the child's winning. It never inherits statement (each child says its own), parts, subtasks, baseline, or the parent's generate: {command}.

    Children share the parent's out/ and workbench/; each child's record is <parent>/<child>. subtasks: decompose lets the orchestrator divide the problem (at most max_subtasks, default 4), and budget.max_depth (default 3) bounds the nesting.

    Composing the whole¶

    Once every child has decided, the parent's generator command composes their designs into the whole. {parts} is a JSON file mapping each child's name to the path of its decided design:

    import json, sys
parts = json.load(open(sys.argv[1]))                       # {"recip": "/.../recip.sv", ...}
open(sys.argv[2], "w").write("\n".join(open(p).read() for p in parts.values()))

    The whole goes straight to the measurements; it is not checked again, so write and test the composition as carefully as a generator. Without a composition command, the parent composes nothing. A parent that generates with a model or agent can instead draft for its children.

    The composition contract has the details, and the NLU example (flux/applications/nlu in the source) shows a larger loop split into child folders.
